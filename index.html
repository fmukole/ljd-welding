Who We Are

Cape Town's Trusted Industrial Welding & Fabrication Partner

LJD Welding is a South African welding and fabrication company based in Kuils River, Cape Town. We specialise in industrial welding, structural steel, custom fabrication, refurbishment, repairs and on-site welding services for clients across the Western Cape.

From single brackets to full structural installations, our qualified welders combine precision engineering with proper safety practice to deliver work that is strong, accurate and built to last — on time, every time.
