About LJD Welding

Professional welding, fabrication and industrial installation, based in Kuils River, Cape Town.

Our Story

Precision Metalwork, Rooted in Cape Town

LJD Welding is a South African welding and fabrication company based in Kuils River, Cape Town. We provide professional welding, custom fabrication, refurbishment and industrial installation services to manufacturing plants, workshops, warehouses, commercial properties and private clients across the Western Cape.

What started as hands-on welding work has grown into a full-service fabrication operation covering everything from structural steel and stainless steel work to mobile on-site repairs. Every project, whether it's a single bracket or a complete steel structure, is approached with the same standard: precise, safe and built to last.

We work directly with engineers, contractors, business owners and home owners to turn drawings, sketches or a simple description into finished steel work you can rely on.
